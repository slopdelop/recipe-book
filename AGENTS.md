# Recipe imagery

Use the `recipe-food-images` skill when sourcing, restoring or generating recipe photographs. It is installed in the user's Codex skills folder. Read `IMAGE-NOTES.md` for source and transformation history before replacing an image.

Favor authentic recipe or brand photographs, and vary each dish's setting and composition thoughtfully. Consistency means realistic food and suitable photographic quality; avoid repeatedly using the same neutral plate and beige tabletop. Verify both collection and hero crops, and credit source photographs and AI alterations accurately.

# Recipe methods

Set `toTry: true` in the recipe data for recipes identified as untried or unvetted. This places them in Recipes to Try. The main collection contains recipes without that flag.

The user wants the full ingredients and method, with only the introductory story removed. Do not shorten, summarize or simplify recipes. Preserve every preparation instruction, burner setting, oven temperature, timing, doneness cue, equipment detail, finishing instruction, serving suggestion, storage note and make-ahead note. Reformatting and step headings are fine; shortening the instructions is not. Omit ads, navigation, nutrition panels and unrelated prose. Preserve user-requested adaptations and label them explicitly; do not silently change quantities, timing or ingredients. If the original source cannot be checked, record that limitation instead of claiming verification or inventing missing directions. Preserve existing recipe, step and timer IDs when correcting instructions so saved data remains attached.

Preserve the original source wording for ingredients, methods and practical recipe notes. Do not paraphrase or rewrite for style, even when the meaning seems equivalent. Formatting, step headings, ingredient highlighting and timer controls may be added without changing the source text. Occasionally add guidance when it is very helpful, but clearly distinguish it as an added note rather than presenting it as part of the original recipe. Keep additions restrained. User-requested adaptations remain authorized and must be identified explicitly. Apply this rule to future recipe additions and edits; do not rewrite existing recipes merely to enforce it unless requested.
